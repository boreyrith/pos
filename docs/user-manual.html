POS User Manual

How to sell, take payment, look after stock, buy from vendors, read the reports and run the system. Written for everyone who uses the till and the back office.

Covers sell screen, catalog, inventory, purchasing, reports, settings Languages English and ខ្មែរ Currencies USD (base) and Khmer riel

01Getting started

Everyone

Signing in

  1. Open the POS address your manager gave you (for example http://shop-pc.local:3000) in Chrome, Edge or Safari, on a computer, tablet or phone.
  2. Under the Sign in button, choose English or ខ្មែរ if you want to change the language.
  3. Enter your Username and Password, then press Sign in.

You land on the first page your role can use: the Sell screen for cashiers, the dashboard or catalog for others.

Good to know
  • First sign-in: if an administrator created your account or reset your password, you must choose your own password (at least 8 characters) before you can continue. Type the password you were given as the current password.
  • Wrong passwords: after 5 wrong passwords in a row the account locks for 15 minutes. An administrator can unlock it sooner.
  • Staying signed in: you stay signed in on that device for up to 7 days. Closing the browser does not sign you out, so always press Sign out on a shared computer.

Changing the language

Everything on screen, including messages, reports and printed receipts, is available in English and Khmer. English is the default.

  • Press the language button in the side bar (it shows the other language's name), or open My account (your initials in the side bar) and use the language switch.
  • The change is instant and is saved to your account, so the same language follows you to any till or phone.
  • Names you or your colleagues typed (products, customers, vendors, notes) stay as they were entered. Products also carry a Khmer name, shown under the English one.

Branches and storages

The business can have several branches (shops), for example Main Shop and Siem Reap. Each branch has one or more storages: places where stock is kept, such as Showroom, Back Store or Warehouse. One storage per branch is the selling storage: the till sells from it.

  • Most staff work for their own branch and see only its stock and sales.
  • People with the All branches permission click the branch code in the side bar to switch branch. Sales, stock and the catalog then act for the branch they pick.

The sell screen: what you can sell on the left, the sale you are building on the right.

Adding items to the sale

There are four ways to add something:

  • Tap a tile. Each tile shows the picture, SKU, English and Khmer name, price and a stock badge. Tapping again adds one more.
  • Scan a barcode with a USB or Bluetooth scanner. The scanner types the code into the search box and presses Enter, and the item is added.
  • Search. Press / to jump to the search box, type part of a name or SKU and press Enter. If exactly one thing matches, it is added.
  • Scan with the camera (phone or tablet): press the scan icon at the right of the search box and hold the barcode or QR code inside the square, 15–25 cm away. Scanning continues until you close it, so you can scan several items.

Narrow the tiles with the type buttons (All, Products, Services, Items, Packages) and the category chips.

Stock limits

You cannot sell more than the selling storage holds. Tiles show 3 left when stock is low and Out when there is none (the tile is greyed out). For a package, the badge shows how many can be made from the stock of what is inside. Units sitting in your other carts count as taken.

Working with the cart

  • Quantity: use + and − on each line. At 1, the minus button becomes a bin and removes the line.
  • Services show extra fields. Done by records who did the work (it defaults to you). Hourly services also ask for Hours, which must be filled before you can charge.
  • Customer: leave it empty for the Walk-in Customer, or search by name, phone or code. If they are new and your role allows it, choose New customer to add them on the spot.
  • Note: anything the order should carry, such as “gift, please wrap” or “pick up at 5pm”.
  • Clear empties the cart after asking you to confirm.

Three carts at once

The tabs Cart 1, Cart 2 and Cart 3 let you keep up to three sales open, for example while one customer fetches another size. Switch with a click or Alt+1/2/3. A cart with items shows a dot. Carts stay put if you move to another page or reload, on that browser tab. To park a sale for longer, or so another till can finish it, use Hold.

Discounts

Order discount

Below the lines, choose % or $ and enter the value. It applies to the whole sale after any item discounts. A percentage cannot exceed 100.

Item discount

Press Discount under a line to take a % or an amount off that line only. This needs the Discount items permission, and each role has a maximum: Managers up to 20%, Cashiers none, Administrators no limit. The dialog shows what the line becomes and refuses more than your role allows.

How the total is worked out

Item discounts come off first, then the order discount (on what is left), then VAT is added or worked out of the price, depending on the store's tax mode. The total on screen is the total the customer pays.

Changing a price

People with Override prices can tap the price under a line to change it, for example for a damaged item or a price match.

  1. Tap the price, enter the new price for one unit (or one hour).
  2. Pick a quick reason (Damaged or near expiry, Price match, Bulk deal, Loyal customer, Wrong label price) or type your own. A reason is required.
  3. Press Apply price. The line shows the catalog price crossed out and the reason.

Prices below the item's cost need the extra Sell below cost permission. Every price change is written to the order and the audit log.

03Taking payment

CashierManager

Cash, in dollars and riel

  1. Press Cash. The dialog shows the total in USD and in riel at today's rate.
  2. Enter what the customer hands over, in one currency or a mix (for example $10 plus 20,000 ៛). Exact fills in the exact amount; the note buttons ($10, $20, $50…) fill a common note.
  3. If more than one currency is accepted, choose Give change in USD or KHR.
  4. The box at the bottom shows Change, or Still to pay in orange if the money is short. Press Complete sale.

The Sale complete dialog shows the change due in large figures and the receipt. Press Print receipt, then New sale.

Riel rounding

Riel amounts are rounded to the nearest 100 ៛, because smaller notes are not in use. A shortfall of less than half a step (under 50 ៛) counts as paid.

KHQR (Bakong)

  1. Press KHQR. If the store accepts riel, choose whether the code charges in USD or KHR.
  2. Turn the screen to the customer. They scan the code with any Bakong-member banking app (ABA, ACLEDA, Wing and others) and pay.
  3. The till checks for the payment every few seconds. When it arrives, the sale completes and the receipt shows.
  • The code is valid for a set time (5 minutes by default), with a countdown under the code. If it expires, the sale is cancelled and the items go back to stock. Press Back to the cart and charge again.
  • If the customer changes their mind, press Cancel payment.
  • While the store is in test mode, a Simulate customer payment (test mode) button appears. No money moves; use it only for training.

The receipt

The receipt is sized for 80 mm receipt printers. It shows:

  • the logo, store details and VAT TIN;
  • the receipt number, which starts with the sale date (for example 20261024-00253);
  • each line with its English and Khmer name, any discount, price change and staff member;
  • totals, VAT, the total in riel at the rate of the sale, and cash or KHQR details with change.

It prints in the language of whoever prints it. Reprint any receipt from the invoice under Sales.

If prices changed

If a price changed while the cart was open (for example a manager edited the catalog), the sale is refused with “Prices changed”. The cart reloads today's prices and lists what changed. Tell the customer the new total and charge again.

04Held sales

CashierManager

Hold a sale when the customer needs time (fetching another size, going to the ATM) or when another till will finish it. Held sales are saved on the server, so they survive a closed browser and any till in the branch can see them.

Holding a sale

  1. With items in the cart, press Hold.
  2. Give it a name so it is easy to find (it suggests the customer's name), for example “Table 4” or “Lady in red dress”, and an optional note.
  3. Press Hold sale. The cart empties and the sale gets a number such as HOLD-00012.

Finding and resuming a held sale

Press Held at the right of the cart tabs. The number on it is how many sales are waiting in your branch. The list shows each sale's number, name, customer, items, total when held, who held it and how long ago.

  • Resume loads it into a free cart with today's prices and stock. A notice lists anything that changed since it was held (new prices, items no longer available).
  • While you have it open it is In use and locked to you, so another till cannot finish it at the same time.
  • Paying it completes it. Hold again saves your changes back. Put back returns it unchanged.
  • Void removes it, with a reason. You can void your own; managers can void anyone's.
Rules
  • A held sale nobody resumes expires after 24 hours. Expired sales appear under Recently closed; only managers can Restore them.
  • If a till leaves a resumed sale open for 30 minutes, the lock is released and the sale is back in the list.
  • A manager can Take over a sale that another till has open.
  • Prices are always checked again when the sale is paid.

05Customers

CashierManagerAccountant (view)

Open Sales › Customers. Every sale belongs to a customer. The built-in Walk-in Customer (code WALK-IN) is used whenever none is picked and cannot be switched off.

  • New customer: name, phone, email, address, VAT TIN (for businesses; printed on receipts) and a note. Customers get a code like CUS-00012.
  • Phone numbers are unique however they are written: “012 345 678” and “012-345-678” are the same number.
  • A customer's page shows their orders, total spent after refunds, refunds, and first and last visit.
  • Untick Active to stop a customer being picked for new sales; their history is kept.

06Invoices, returns and refunds

Cashier (view, cancel)ManagerAccountant (view)

Finding an invoice

Open Sales › Invoices. Search by order number, customer or phone, and filter by status and dates. Open an invoice to see every line, discount, tax, payment, and any returns.

StatusMeaning
CompletedPaid in full.
Awaiting paymentA KHQR code is waiting to be paid.
CancelledNever paid; the stock went back.
Partly refundedSome items were returned and refunded.
RefundedEverything was returned and refunded.

Returning some items

Needs the Refunds and returns permission (Managers and Administrators).

  1. Open the invoice and press Return items.
  2. For each line, enter how many units come back. The screen shows what was paid for them, so the refund matches what the customer actually paid after discounts.
  3. Add a reason (for example “wrong size”), check the refund amount, and press Refund.

The returned stock goes back into the storage the sale came from, and the return gets a number such as RET-00007. Give the money back the way it was paid: cash for cash, bank transfer for KHQR.

Refunding the whole order

Press Refund all (or Refund the rest after a partial return) and confirm. Everything left is refunded and put back into stock. This cannot be undone.

Exchanges

There is no separate exchange screen. Return the item, then sell the replacement as a new sale.

Cancelling an unpaid order

An order Awaiting payment can be cancelled with Cancel order. Its items go back to stock. Paid orders are never cancelled; refund them instead.

All returns are listed under Sales › Returns & refunds, with totals and the tax refunded.

07Catalog

Everyone viewsManager and Administrator edit

Four kinds of entry

Products

Goods you sell and keep in stock, such as a bodysuit 3-pack or a pack of diapers.

Items

Parts and materials. Used by services, such as gift-wrap paper and thread. Some can also be sold at the till.

Services

Work you charge for, such as gift wrapping or name embroidery. Priced per job or per hour.

Packages

Bundles sold at their own price, such as a newborn gift set. Selling one takes the stock of what is inside.

Adding or editing an entry

Choose the tab and press New product (or service, item, package). Then fill in the fields:

  • Name (English) and Name (Khmer). Both are required; the Khmer name prints on receipts and labels.
  • SKU: leave empty to generate one. Barcode: type it, scan it with a scanner, or use Scan with camera.
  • Price and Cost. Cost is what you pay the supplier; reports use it for profit. The label shows whether the price includes VAT.
  • Category, Tax rate, a picture (PNG, JPEG or WebP, shrunk automatically) and a description.
  • Base unit (pcs, pack, pair, set, bottle…) and Pack sizes, such as 1 carton = 4 packs, so you can buy and count in cartons.
  • Low-stock alert at: the dashboard warns when stock falls to this level.
  • Opening stock (new products and items only): it goes into your branch's selling storage.
  • Services: pricing per job or per hour, the usual duration, and Parts used per job. Each sale takes those items out of stock.
  • Packages: under Contents, add products, items, services or other packages. The screen compares the package price with buying everything separately.
  • Active: untick it to hide an entry everywhere without deleting its history.

Changing stock from the catalog

People with Adjust stock can press the stock figure on a product or item, choose the storage and what happened, then save:

  • Receive: goods arrived or were found. The amount is added.
  • Remove: damaged, expired, lost, stolen or used in-house. The amount is taken away, with a reason.
  • Count: you counted the shelf. The count becomes the stock.

For several items at once, use Inventory › Adjustments.

Units and categories

The Units and Categories buttons at the top of the catalog manage the lists of units and categories. A unit in use cannot be deleted. Deleting a category leaves its entries uncategorised.

Filters: search by name, SKU or barcode, pick a category, see stock for this branch or a single storage, Low stock only, and Include hidden.

09Inventory

Inventory staffManager

Stock is kept per storage. Every change, whether a sale, return, delivery, transfer or adjustment, is recorded with who did it and when.

Transfers

Move stock between storages, in the same branch or to another branch.

  1. Inventory › Transfers › New transfer.
  2. Choose From and To storages, search and add the items, and enter the quantities (in units or packs). The screen shows what is available in the source.
  3. Press Send transfer (or Move stock within one branch).
  • Same branch: the stock moves straight away.
  • Another branch: the transfer is In transit until someone at the receiving branch presses Receive. Until then the stock is in neither place.
  • A transfer in transit can be cancelled; the stock goes back to where it came from.

Adjustments

Inventory › Adjustments › New adjustment fixes several items at once. For each line, choose Increase by, Decrease by or Set to, the amount, and a reason:

Stock counts

  1. Under Inventory › Adjustments, start a Count for a storage. You can count just one category or type.
  2. The count sheet lists every product and item with the stock the system expects. Type what you counted, in the base unit. Leave a row blank to keep it as it is.
  3. Use Only differences to review, check the value of the differences, then Apply count.

Selling during a count is fine. The counted number is applied, and any sale made while you counted is recorded separately.

History

Inventory › History lists every stock change: when, the entry, what happened (sale, refund, purchase received, transfer in or out, adjustment), the change and the running balance. Filter by branch, storage and kind.

10Purchasing

Manager (orders)Inventory staff (receive)Accountant (invoices, payments)

The top of Purchasing shows what is owed to vendors, unpaid and overdue invoices, and orders waiting to be received.

Vendors

Add vendor: a short unique code (for example V-BCD), name, contact person, phone, email, VAT TIN, address and payment terms in days (0 = pay on receipt). Inactive vendors cannot receive new orders.

Purchase orders

  1. New purchase order: choose the vendor, where to deliver (a storage), the expected date and the vendor's quote number.
  2. Add products and items with quantity, unit (for example cartons) and cost per unit. Save it as a draft while you are still deciding.
  3. Press Place order when you send it to the vendor. Only drafts can be edited.

Receiving a delivery

  1. Open the order and press Receive delivery.
  2. Enter what actually arrived for each line (it suggests what is still to come), and the delivery note number. Note any problem, such as “2 boxes damaged, refused”.
  3. Optionally tick Update each item's catalog cost to this purchase price.
  4. Press Receive into stock. The stock goes into the order's storage and the delivery gets a number such as GRN-00006.

If the vendor will not send the rest, press Close short to stop waiting for it.

Vendor invoices and payments

  • Record invoice (on the order, or under Invoices & payments): the vendor's invoice number, invoice date, subtotal, tax charged (input VAT) and due date. The due date defaults to the vendor's payment terms.
  • Pay records a payment: amount, date, method (cash, bank transfer, KHQR, cheque, other) and reference. Pay in full or in parts.
  • Statuses: Unpaid, Partly paid, Paid, Void. Unpaid invoices past their due date are marked Overdue.
  • An invoice with payments recorded cannot be voided.

12Users, roles and the audit log

AdministratorManager (view)

Staff accounts

  1. Users › Users › Add user: full name, username (letters, numbers, dots, dashes), email, phone, role and branch.
  2. Set a temporary password of at least 8 characters and give it to them in person. They must choose their own password when they first sign in.

Under Manage for a user you can:

  • edit their details, role and branch;
  • untick Account can sign in to disable the account;
  • Reset password, which also unlocks the account and signs them out everywhere;
  • Unlock an account locked after wrong passwords;
  • Sign out everywhere, for a lost phone or someone who has left.

You cannot change your own role or disable your own account, and you can only give roles within your own permissions.

Roles and permissions

Each person has one role. A role is a set of permissions, and the server checks every action, so a hidden button is never the only protection. Under Users › Roles & permissions you can adjust the built-in roles or create new ones (New role, or Copy as new role). Some permissions bring others with them, such as refunding, which needs viewing orders. Each role also has a maximum item discount.

13Settings

Administrator changesManager and Accountant view

General

Trading name and legal name, logo (printed on receipts, up to 512 KB), VAT TIN, address, phone, email, website, the time zone (which decides the business day for reports and receipt numbers) and the receipt footer, for example your return policy.

Branches & storages

Add branches (a selling storage called Shop floor is created with each) and storages. Choose the selling storage with Make selling. A storage must be empty before it can be deactivated, and the selling storage cannot be deactivated.

Taxes & payments

Tax mode

ModeListed price $10.00, VAT 10%
No VATCustomer pays $10.00; no tax is calculated.
VAT includedCustomer pays $10.00, of which VAT $0.91 (price × rate ÷ (100 + rate)).
VAT setup valueCustomer pays $11.00; $1.00 VAT is added at checkout.

You also set the display name (for example “VAT”), the default rate, a maximum rate, and which types are taxed. Under Tax rates, add rates such as Exempt 0% and assign them to catalog entries. Orders always keep the tax they were sold with; changes only affect new sales.

Currencies

USD is the base currency: prices, totals and reports are in dollars. Turn on other currencies (such as riel) to accept them in cash. Press Update rate whenever the rate changes. New sales use the new rate, and past sales keep theirs. Each currency has a cash step, the smallest amount handed over (100 for riel).

KHQR payments

  • Test (simulated): QR codes are shown but no money moves. For training and setup.
  • Live (Bakong): real payments. You need the store's Bakong account ID (for example shopname@aclb), the merchant name and city shown in the customer's app, and a Bakong API token. Use Test connection to check the token. Tokens expire after 90 days and must be renewed on the Bakong API portal.
  • How long a code stays valid (minutes).

System

A read-only page for whoever runs the server. It shows the version, database, session lengths, sign-in lockout, held-sale and KHQR timers, and background jobs. Items marked with a warning need attention before the system goes live, such as the development signing key or HTTPS-only cookies being off. These settings are changed on the server, not here.

Things the system never allows
  • Selling more than is in stock, or stock below zero.
  • Cancelling a paid order (refund it instead), or refunding more than was paid.
